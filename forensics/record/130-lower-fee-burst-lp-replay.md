# Lower-fee LP after fast Robinhood pool bursts

This replay extends the [Pons-inclusive birth screen](120-pons-hook-and-burst-universe.md). A `4/300` or `5/600` signal requires the corresponding number of distinct quote-pair v4 pools to have **positive net liquidity**, observed by the end of the signal block. Zero-hook and Pons-hooked pools can contribute to that count; unrelated hooks are excluded. The proposed LP destinations are separate zero-hook, static-fee quote pools already known at the signal block. The Pons pool's fee is paid through its hook to the launchpad's protocol/creator/buyback distribution, so its swaps do not give a third-party LP core fee income. [Pons v2 pool accounting](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2#uniswap-v4-pools)

The replay places an independent hypothetical **$4 quote-only position in every eligible destination** whose adjacent one-tick range is quote-only at the completed signal-block price and remains so at a two-block-later mint. It follows observed historical swaps, assigns a share of core LP fees to that hypothetical position, and exits at the first full-token conversion, return to full quote after activation, own-pool liquidity below 75% of its observed peak, or 30 minutes. The earliest modeled burn is the next block. The mint model assumes our mint precedes that block's historical swaps; exact transaction ordering is unproven. The 75% level, two-block delay, range width, and time cap are assumptions for testing, not fitted optimal parameters.

| Strict funded rule | Valid $4 destinations | Activated in-band | Historical exit-spot fee mark | Sampled add/remove gas proxy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `4/300` | 88 across 21 tokens | 9 | $2.08 | $3.16 |
| `5/600` | 109 across 20 tokens | 7 | $1.46 | $3.91 |

The two rules overlap in tokens and destinations; their rows must **not** be added together as a portfolio. All-destination median fee yield is zero for both. These sums are **fees only at the historical spot mark**; they omit position principal conversion, route impact, ETH/USDG exchange cost, and liquidation gas. They are not profits. The $4 stake is independent for each pool, so this table is not a cash-constrained $104 wallet replay.

The [compact lower-fee result](../results/v4-burst-low-fee-strict-summary-24h.json) preserves each of the 54 strict funded below-70%-fee destination cases, including its token, pool, signal block, exit reason, principal spot mark, and fee spot mark. It can be regenerated from the full local replay with [the export script](../export_v4_burst_fee_summary.py).

Restricting the destination to fee tiers **below 70%** leaves 21 destinations across eight tokens for `4/300`: three activate, with **$1.35** total historical spot-marked fees against **$0.75** sampled add/remove gas. For `5/600`, 33 destinations across 11 tokens yield six activations and **$0.75** spot-marked fees against **$1.18** sampled add/remove gas. The single largest token contributes most of the `4/300` fee mark. **None of the 21 `4/300` lower-fee positions exceeded $4 after modeled principal plus fees at exit spot, before gas.** Three of the 33 `5/600` positions had spot marks above $4 plus the sampled mint/burn gas; those still require an executable liquidation route. A fee mark exceeding gas does not establish that the withdrawn launch token can be sold for that mark.

One causal historical-block fork tested that concern for a 50%-fee USDG pool after its `4/300` signal. A quote-only LP minted **$2.597400 USDG** into its proposed band. Replaying the exact signed historical swap after injection and immediately burning returned **$0.664301 USDG plus 4,872,133.962197 launch tokens**. Fee growth accounted for about 2,436,066.981098 of those tokens. A local same-pool quote sold the entire token amount for **$0.779726 USDG**, giving **$1.444027 total versus $2.597400 invested**, a **$1.153373 gross loss before gas**. All 15 same-token pools in the cached medium/high-fee set were checked; none improved the single-pool quote. This fork validates one actual post-signal route, not all positions or all possible routes, and its immediate burn differs from the full exit rule.

For the three `5/600` lower-fee positions that cleared $4 plus sampled mint/burn gas at the historical spot mark, same-pool **observed-state** Quoter calls at the modeled burn blocks returned $4.313279, $4.037903, and $4.075958 against a $4 entry, before gas. These calls use the modeled withdrawn inventory against the unaltered historical pool. They do not replay the swaps after injecting our liquidity; the 50%-fee fork above shows that this distinction can change the outcome substantially. They also omit token-sale gas.

**Result so far:** the larger Pons-inclusive burst universe supplied 21 strict funded `4/300` signals and 20 strict funded `5/600` signals, a union of 23 tokens in this 24-hour sample. This LP construction has no demonstrated positive executable return. The fixed-history fee marks assume that injecting our $4 position does not change the subsequent price path. The next economic gate is a causal fork of the promising-at-spot `5/600` routes under the full exit timing, then a cash-constrained wallet path. Only after those checks could a stake fraction or expected wallet profitability be reported credibly.
