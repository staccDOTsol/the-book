# Pons every-launch quote pools: discovery, ranges, and gas

The proposed system is distinct from the [NOTHINGBURGER counterfactual replay](150-nothingburger-counterfactual-replay.md). It would open one independent Uniswap v4 `X/Q` pool for each newly launched Pons token `X`, start with **one strategy-owned LP position** (the earlier three-range idea remains a parameter sweep), withdraw after a fixed delay (use **60 minutes** as the first test parameter), liquidate `X`, burn recovered quote token `Q`, and distribute ETH. No such strategy was deployed or traded here.

## How to find the launches

Pons V2 begins on a bonding curve. The factory `0x7eD598BcEf8bd9Edd8C97A195C6d13f40801EC7e` emits `TokenLaunched(token, curve, deployer, pairToken, launchConfigId, graduationThreshold)` at creation. A Pons-hook `PoolRegistered` event and a v4 `Initialize` happen only at **graduation**, too late for a launch-time strategy. The factory exposes `getLaunchedToken(token)` to verify a *supplied* address; it stores launches in a mapping and exposes no iterable token list. The hook and v4 PoolManager likewise have mappings and events, not an onchain linked list of launches. A contract cannot read past EVM logs. [Pons event guide](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2#events-to-index), [factory source](https://github.com/ponsdotdev/pons-labs/blob/main/contractsV2/src/v2/PonsV2LaunchFactory.sol), [PoolManager source](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol).

Use a block-cursor watcher of the factory's `TokenLaunched` logs, with websocket notification and `eth_getLogs` backfill after reconnect. The watcher's **only write** is `enqueue(token)` signed by the owner; the contract verifies `getLaunchedToken(token).exists` and deduplicates it. Put fresh launches on a **LIFO entry stack**, so the next transfer works on the newest token. Each item's stage is `QUEUED → POOL_INITIALIZED → FUNDED → CLOSED`. Keep a separate deadline-ordered exit cursor for funded positions; each eligible quote-token transfer attempts **one** stage, prioritizing an exit already due before popping the latest entry. A failed stage stays retryable and must not permanently block later launches. For a first test, an exit becomes due **60 minutes** after funding. A permissionless `processNext()` provides a fallback if transfers stop. Strict LIFO for exits would let a constant flow of new positions postpone old exits indefinitely.

An ERC-20 transfer cannot discover a launch that was never enqueued. It also pays for its own attempted stage: creating a pool and LP position can cost hundreds of thousands of gas, making ordinary token transfers expensive. Calls into the v4 position manager can themselves transfer `Q`; the token needs a strict in-progress guard to prevent recursive stage execution. Transfers occurring inside another v4 settlement may be unable to open a nested v4 operation, so stage failure should be caught without reverting the user's transfer. Time passing does not execute a transaction by itself. With **5,767 launches/day**, separate initialize, fund, and close stages require at least **17,301 successful triggering transfers/day**, about **12/minute**, before retries; a lower transfer rate builds a backlog. Batching pool initialization and LP funding into one stage would reduce this to about **8/minute** including exit.

## Token and range mechanics

[Pools.xyz](https://support.uniswap.org/hc/en-us/articles/47943121516685-Launching-and-trading-tokens-on-pools-xyz) launches its fixed token implementation with permanently locked launch liquidity. It is not a deployment path for an arbitrary ERC-20 `_transfer` implementation or three positions that the creator withdraws later. A custom `Q` would need a separate deployment, and its `X/Q` pools and positions would be created directly through [Uniswap v4 PoolManager and PositionManager](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/guides/create-pool), owned by a strategy vault. A standard Pools.xyz token could be `Q` in separate strategy-owned pools, but then orchestration still belongs in the vault, not its token transfer code.

For the currently observed Pons curve terms (1.68 ETH phantom quote, 4.2 ETH graduation threshold), the theoretical graduation *spot* is **12.25×** the initial spot. The proposed upper targets are therefore 12.25×, 24.5×, and 122.5× the launch spot, before repricing for movements in `Q/ETH`. `getReserves()` and fee/snipe-tax state determine executable curve quotes; the marginal ratio alone is not a swap quote. [Pons curve guide](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2#getting-a-quote).

The funding side is decisive. If v4 price is `Q per X`, then at the **lower boundary** of an upward `[launch, graduation]` position, the position is **X-only**. It becomes `Q` as `X` rises. A position seeded with `Q` alone sits below the current price and buys `X` on a *decline*; it cannot be the requested upward ladder at launch. Funding three upward ranges requires buying/depositing `X` first, plus enough `Q` for any range placed in the money. Minting `Q` freely does not itself give it an ETH price or create arbitrage demand. An independently initialized `X/Q` pool needs an executable external `Q/ETH` price to set sensible ticks. Neither swap activity nor a fee automatically guarantees positive ETH proceeds after inventory changes, burning, and gas.

## Exit and distribution

The proposed proceeds rule is: withdraw all strategy positions; sell half of recovered `X` for ETH and half for `Q`; burn the `Q` obtained from the sale and any returned `Q`; split the resulting ETH equally between the wizard fanout and developer. On that definition, each recipient gets **25% of gross `X` liquidation value**, while the other 50% is spent buying and burning `Q`, before fees, slippage, and gas. Route `X` to its live Pons curve while it is open and to its graduated v4 pool after phase 2; the temporary swept phase must wait or retry. Place minimum-output and deadline bounds on both sales.

Squarefun's current Robinhood wizard fanout is `0x1b88A6c6516FD2918905186F21Bb9F5CaA1a15c8`. Its [verified `FeeFanout` source/ABI](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/4663/0x1b88A6c6516FD2918905186F21Bb9F5CaA1a15c8?fields=abi,sources) accepts ERC-20 balances for harvest/claim and has no payable ETH receiver. Wrap the wizard half of the ETH to WETH and transfer WETH to that contract; send the developer half as ETH to a configured recipient. Reverify the fanout address from the live Squarefun frontend before deployment.

## One-day scale and sampled transaction cost

In the exact 24-hour replay window (Robinhood blocks **77,940,490–78,796,050**), the factory emitted **5,767 `TokenLaunched` events**, about **4.00 launches/minute**: 4,628 paired with native ETH, 228 with USDG, and 911 with other ERC-20 assets. Only **52** Pons pools graduated during that window; that is a different, much smaller event stream. An every-launch rule requests **5,767 pools plus either 5,767 or 17,301 LP positions per day**, regardless of whether anyone later trades them. Non-ETH launch pairs require an additional executable path to ETH for the proposed payout. [Launch cadence data](../results/pons-v2-launch-cadence-24h.json).

One observed zero-hook ETH-pair [pool creation and one LP mint transaction](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/tx/0x863453b8e1918868ab51fcf659389cd771cc80d786dda25238eddd2bc34294de) used **417,375 gas** at **0.034794 gwei**, or **0.00001452214575 ETH**. Multiplying that one receipt by 5,767 gives **0.08375 ETH ($217.75 at $2,600/ETH)** for openings. The median of ten sampled standalone LP removals used **149,821 gas** at a **0.03213 gwei** median price; multiplying gives another **0.02776 ETH ($72.18)** for one timed withdrawal per pool. Combined, about **0.11151 ETH ($289.93)** for one-position open/close. This is an illustrative historical-price extrapolation, not a gas estimate for a custom vault.

For three positions, combining that initial pool-plus-one-position receipt with two median standalone mints and three median removals gives roughly **0.28321 ETH ($736.34)** for one day of opens/closes. Batching three mints or burns could change the gas materially. Neither estimate includes contract deployment, launch indexing/queue calls, quote-token acquisition, swaps, wrapping WETH, or payout execution. The previously proposed **$104 = 0.04 ETH** wallet would be exhausted by the one-position open/close extrapolation if every launch were processed, even with no trades.
