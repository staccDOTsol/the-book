# Litigation — Aguilar v. Baton

Public docket summary.

**Case:** Aguilar v. Baton Corporation Ltd. d/b/a Pump.Fun et al., No. 1:25-cv-00880, S.D.N.Y.
**Judge:** Colleen McMahon.
**Plaintiffs' counsel:** Burwick Law.

## Posture

- Filed 2025-01-30.
- Second amended consolidated class action complaint filed 2026-01-07.
- **2026-08-31, motion-to-dismiss opinion ([184]):** the RICO claims proceed against Baton, its founders, and 25 unidentified promoters. The court found the complaint plausibly alleges the enterprise, with predicate acts including wire fraud, illegal gambling, and operating an unlicensed money-transmitting business, and that certain participants received pre-promotion information and sold into the demand they generated. Securities Act and unjust-enrichment claims, and the claims against the Solana defendants, were dismissed.
- **Answers due 2026-10-15.** Discovery follows.
