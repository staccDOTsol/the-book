All times below are **2026-10-03 UTC**. Its first Uniswap v4 pool appeared at **01:30:53**, with a zero fee. The first static high-fee pool was #2 at 01:32:00. The third, fourth, and fifth distinct pools appeared at 01:33:08, 01:33:28, and 01:34:38. No three-, four-, or five-pool rule could have signaled during the chart's 01:31 minute.

The replay checks each `Initialize` pool birth and its completed block for a static fee of **at least 70% and below 100%** plus positive net `ModifyLiquidity`. It then freezes the pools known at entry and records the first later block where their aggregate net liquidity falls **below 75% of its observed peak**. The initial zero-fee pool is excluded from that exit proxy. PoolManager liquidity units are not USD, in-range liquidity, or proof of common ownership. The [chain event replay](../results/launch-threshold-replay.json) records every first crossing from 95% to 50% remaining.

The [verified print replay](../results/launch-verified-replay.json) selected the first regular third-party trade of at least $4 and at least five seconds after each event. Each selected trade occurred in a later block; Robinhood v4 `Swap` receipt signs verified token buy at entry and token sell at exit. **All eight selected prints used the separate zero-fee base pool**, rather than the new high-fee pools. Entry trade sizes ranged from about $13 to $2,276 and exit sizes from about $4.88 to $498. These price ratios are **not executable $4 Fomo returns**, and the high-fee LP cannot be credited with causing the base-pool price moves.

Pool #2 is especially weak as a funding signal. Its 98.9999%-fee Initialize transaction transferred only **0.021960 USDG and 4,391.624 LAUNCH** to PoolManager: about **$0.043920 at that pool's quoted ratio**, or roughly **$0.518** if the LAUNCH tokens are valued at the later verified base-pool print. It fails even a $1 total-deposit floor. Pool #3 and #5 had roughly $19.01 and $11.45 of two-sided deposits at their own USDG/LAUNCH ratios; pool #4 had LAUNCH plus native ETH whose exact USD settlement was not established here. The [deposit receipts](../results/launch-initial-deposits.json) preserve the transfer amounts and sizing assumptions. Positive net LP alone is not enough to call the #2 pool a usable venue.

A separate, much later **actual** LAUNCH $4 round trip was [opened and closed on Fomo](https://fomo.family/tokens/robinhood/0x6c7c3113bfa9eef3e716a4912d9b0dc47aefe796?tradeId=843c1844-44b8-45eb-9ba5-fa3358774b1e) at 04:12:15–04:12:19 UTC. The wallet spent **4.000000 USDC** and received **3.500279 USDC** back, a **−$0.499721 (−12.49%) cash result**. Fomo reported a different $3.86304 cost basis and −$0.411599 realized PnL. Applying that one trade's 0.87506975 cash-retention factor to the historical print ratios would be only a sensitivity calculation; fees, routes, liquidity, timing, and fill odds differed. One token cannot establish expected profitability or a rule to automate.
